# Scenario checks

Why each proposition is not supported by its facts.

- **example-finance-001** (false): 80% sub-investment-grade bonds, a 14% fall in 2022 that took 16 months to recover, and no capital guarantee mean 18 months is not safe from loss.
- **example-consumer-001** (unsupported): Rated for 15 m2, not 25 m2, and the maker has run no study of any effect on allergy or asthma symptoms.
- **pilot-finance-002** (unsupported): The 100% repayment applies only at maturity (5 years); early sale is at market price, and buy-back prices were 4-11% below cost in the first three years.
- **pilot-finance-003** (false): 5.4% lasts only three months, then 1.9%, and any month with a withdrawal or under $500 paid in earns 0.5%.
- **pilot-consumer-002** (false): The comfort rating is +2 C and the limit rating -4 C; -15 C is a survival-only extreme rating, far from comfort at -12 C.
- **finance-004** (unsupported): A 71% drawdown not recovered within 22 months and four 30% falls in five years, with no protection, mean a three-year horizon gives no assurance the capital is intact.
- **finance-005** (false): Each missed payment adds a $25 fee, so missing one or two payments makes the total exceed $2,400 by definition.
- **finance-006** (unsupported): The 8% is a non-guaranteed target, arrears tripled to 6.8% and the provision fund is discretionary, so a dependable $4,000 a year is not supported.
- **finance-007** (false): A 3% fee ($240) is charged on transfer, and $230 x 15 months repays only $3,450, leaving a balance charged at 27.9%.
- **finance-008** (false): Aldermoor's own illustration shows a policy surrendered after eight years returns about 78% of premiums, which is less than was paid in.
- **finance-009** (false): Money is locked in for at least 30 months, and only 22% of stakes listed for sale found a buyer within three months.
- **finance-010** (unsupported): Fernway's own guidance caps single-sector funds at 10% near retirement, and the ETF is concentrated and fell 47% in 2022.
- **finance-011** (false): Pre-existing conditions are excluded unless declared, Essentials cannot take declarations, and diabetes is a pre-existing condition.
- **consumer-003** (unsupported): The seat installs only with Isofix and a support leg, the fitting list starts at 2011, the user's car is not on it, and the maker forbids use in unlisted cars.
- **consumer-004** (unsupported): The 80 km is a best case; hills cut range up to 40% and sub-zero cold a further 20-30%, which puts winter hilly range well under 70 km.
- **consumer-005** (unsupported): The only trial found no significant difference from placebo, and the maker disclaims any treatment effect; reviews are not evidence of efficacy.
- **consumer-006** (false): Without the $4.99 subscription the camera saves no recordings, and it has no local storage.
- **consumer-007** (false): The manual explicitly says it must not be used for scuba diving, and 5 ATM is a static rating, not a 50-metre depth rating.
- **consumer-008** (false): The manual forbids use while asleep or unattended and requires one metre from beds, and the heater has no tip-over switch.
- **consumer-009** (false): 8 GB fixed memory and integrated graphics fall far below Resolve's 4K recommendations, and a review saw dropped frames on a 4K timeline.
